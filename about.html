Our Mandate

1. Act as the primary national government entity responsible for the management of housing, human settlements and urban development.

2. Be the sole and main planning and policy-making, regulatory, program coordination, and performance monitoring entity for all housing, human settlement and urban development concerns, primarily focusing on the access to and the affordability of basic human needs. For this purpose, the following functions of HLURB are hereby transferred to the Department:

  • The land use planning and monitoring function, including the imposition of penalties for noncompliance to ensure that LGUs will follow the planning guidelines and implement their CLUPs and ZOs;
  • The regulatory function, including the formulation, promulgation, and enforcement of rules, standards and guidelines over subdivisions, condominiums and similar real estate developments, and imposition of fines and other administrative sanctions for violations;
  • The registration, regulation and supervision of HOAs, including the imposition of fines for violations.

3. Develop and adopt a national strategy to immediately address the provision of adequate and affordable housing to all Filipinos, and ensure the alignment of the policies, programs, and projects of all its attached agencies to facilitate the achievement of this objective.

DHSUD – Region 6 (Western Visayas)

The Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) operates as the primary government entity responsible for management, policy-making, and housing regulation in the Philippines. DHSUD Region 6 serves as the regional executive arm covering the provinces of Aklan, Antique, Capiz, Guimaras, Iloilo, and Negros Occidental. Based in Iloilo City, the regional office translates national housing and spatial policies into localized development.


Core Responsibilities:

  • Regional Housing Program Management: Coordinates the implementation of national government housing projects (such as socialized and economic housing) tailored to Western Visayas.
  • Local Governance Support: Assists Local Government Units (LGUs) across Region 6 in establishing local housing boards and updating spatial planning frameworks.
  • Regulatory Services: Handles the licensing and regulation of real estate developers, subdivisions, condominiums, and Homeowners Associations (HOAs) within the region.
  • Disaster Resilience Planning: Ensures regional urban planning incorporates climate resilience and risk reduction measures, particularly critical given Western Visayas' exposure to natural disasters.

Environmental, Land Use and Urban Planning and Development Division (ELUPDD)

ELUPDD is a core division within DHSUD regional offices (operating under the broader technical umbrella of the national ELUPD Bureau). It handles spatial planning, environmental integration, and land utilization.


Key Responsibilities:

AreaPrimary Functions
CLUP Review & AssistanceReviews and provides technical assistance for LGUs' Comprehensive Land Use Plans (CLUPs) and Zoning Ordinances (ZOs) to ensure alignment with national standards.
CDRA IntegrationOversees the integration of Climate Change Adaptation and Disaster Risk Reduction Assessment (CDRA) into local land use policies.
Urban Policy & GISConducts spatial analyses, mapping (GIS), and policy formulation to promote sustainable urban expansion and land use efficiency.
LGU Capacity BuildingConducts trainings and workshops for local planners and officers to ensure effective spatial planning and zoning enforcement.
