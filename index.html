ELUPDD

The Environmental, Land Use, and Urban Planning and Development Division (ELUPDD)—along with its national counterpart, the Bureau (ELUPDB)—is a core division under the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) in the Philippines. It leads the country's initiatives in human settlements planning, urban development policies, and spatial land allocation.
